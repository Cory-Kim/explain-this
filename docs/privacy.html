Explain This Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Explain This is operated by Dokyung Kim. It lets you photograph or choose an image, receive an AI explanation, ask follow-up questions, and save conversations on your device. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact cdokyung@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Photos and questions: When you request an explanation, a compressed copy of the selected photo, your language choice, and explanation style are sent through our Cloudflare backend to Google's Gemini API. Follow-up requests send the image, your question, the selected language, and the previous explanation.
  • Installation identifier: The app creates a random identifier, stores it on your device, and sends it to our backend to identify the installation. It is not an account or authentication credential. Our current backend does not save new usage-counter records.
  • Connection information: Cloudflare processes network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Its infrastructure can infer approximate location from an IP address. The app does not request GPS location, and our application code does not use inferred location to generate explanations.
  • Privacy correspondence: If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use them to respond to your request.

Google Gemini and Cloudflare

Google processes submitted content to generate answers. This version is configured for Gemini's unpaid service. Under Google's unpaid-service terms, submitted images, prompts, and generated responses may be used to provide and improve Google's products and machine-learning technologies, and human reviewers may process them. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information, including private documents or identifiable photos of people.

See Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy for Google's processing and retention practices.

Cloudflare hosts our backend and processes traffic to operate and secure its services. Persistent Worker logs and traces are currently disabled; this does not disable Cloudflare's separate network processing. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Data may be processed outside your country.

Saved history and retention

Saved history is stored locally on your device and includes compressed image thumbnails, explanations, follow-up questions and answers, language, style, and dates. The app keeps up to 20 conversations, subject to a storage-size limit; older entries may be removed when those limits are reached. Explain This does not provide account-based cloud history synchronization. Device or operating-system backups may be governed by your device settings.

Our backend code does not write uploaded photos, questions, or AI answers to a database. They pass through the backend to service requests. This does not mean Google or Cloudflare process all data only in memory or immediately delete it. Their retention practices are governed by the policies linked above. Legacy usage records, if present, are scheduled for deletion once older than two days.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can delete individual saved conversations or clear history in the app. Clearing the app's storage also removes its local history and installation identifier. These actions do not remove original photos from your photo library or data already processed by service providers.

You can deny or revoke camera permission in device settings and choose which images to provide. Selecting a photo alone does not send it to Gemini; requesting an explanation or a follow-up does. Using online AI features requires sending the request data described above.

To request deletion of information we control, email cdokyung@gmail.com with the subject “Explain This privacy request.” Describe the request without including sensitive photos. We will assess what information we can identify and delete, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot remotely erase local history or promise deletion of provider-held data outside our control; we will explain relevant limitations.

Security, accounts, and advertising

The production app uses HTTPS to communicate with our backend, and the backend uses HTTPS to communicate with Gemini. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. The app has no user-account creation, advertising, or in-app purchases. We do not sell your information or use it to target advertising.

Age eligibility

Explain This is intended for adults aged 18 and over. People under 18 should not use its Gemini-powered features. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us.

Policy changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may also be communicated within the app.
